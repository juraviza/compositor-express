# Freebeat forum tips and public creator patterns

## Strongest repeated positives

- Freebeat is strongest when beat sync matters.
- It performs well for dance clips, performance clips, lyric videos, and short-form music promos.
- Users often like the speed from song to watchable draft.

## Strongest repeated warnings

- Results are hit or miss if prompts are vague.
- Full-song renders can burn credits quickly.
- Faces and character consistency can drift if the user does not prepare references.
- Long videos feel weaker when the whole song is treated as one undirected block.

## Best-practice patterns repeated by users

### Start short
- Test 15 to 30 seconds first.
- Start with the chorus, drop, or hook.
- Validate style before spending on the full song.

### Split the song
Use separate visual logic for:
- intro
- verse
- pre
- chorus
- bridge
- outro

### Regenerate surgically
- Keep the good scenes.
- Replace only the weak ones.
- Do not restart from zero unless the visual direction is wrong.

### Prepare characters
For singer or avatar workflows:
- use 1 or 2 characters max
- use prepared source images when possible
- keep wardrobe and visual identity fixed

### Use structured prompts
Include:
- subject
- environment
- emotional tone
- camera behavior
- lighting/palette
- style
- exclusions

## Practical heuristics

### For professional-looking output
- prefer one main style per section
- keep the color palette controlled
- use cleaner scenes under dense lyrics
- ask for cinematic camera movement, not chaos

### For social clips
- prefer 9:16
- build around one memorable visual hook
- let the strongest lyric or musical moment dominate the clip

### For YouTube or premium feel
- prefer 16:9
- use stronger narrative continuity
- pace scene changes by musical structure, not just by novelty

## Useful critique checklist after a render

Check these in order:
1. Does the visual mood match the song?
2. Does the strongest musical moment get the strongest image?
3. Are the faces stable?
4. Are scene transitions coherent?
5. Are lyrics readable?
6. Is there any scene that looks generic, broken, or off-brand?

## Operator stance

Treat Freebeat as an AI director that still needs direction.
The best results come from guided iteration, not one-shot generation.
